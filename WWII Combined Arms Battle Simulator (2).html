🎖️ Combined Arms Operations Headquarters

Operations room

Build two armies from infantry classes, armour and air groups, pick terrain and weather, then watch the battle unfold on a live map. Armies are simulated as formations (up to 12 per unit type), so any quantity you enter is handled by scaling formation size, not by simulating each soldier.

Combat uses penetration vs. armour, range and visibility, terrain, weather, morale, experience, commander skill, supply and air superiority. Gameplay values are balancing figures inspired by historical data.

Battlefield editor

Pick a tile and click or drag to paint. Traps belong to the chosen owner and only hurt the enemy: mines hurt infantry, anti-tank mines hurt vehicles, barbed wire slows infantry and trenches protect their owner. Place unit adds a formation of the chosen type and size where you click, and if an army has placed units only those fight for it. Frontline A and B: click points from top to bottom to draw each army start line, and its units deploy along it. Then tick Use custom battlefield on the Create battle screen.
